The 26 Blue Sharks of World Cup 2026

Named on May 18, 2026, with Ryan Mendes as captain and Vozinha as vice-captain, Cabo Verde's first-ever World Cup squad drew heavily on the nation's large European diaspora.

Head Coach: Bubista (Pedro Leitão Brito)

A former Cabo Verde international who has led the national team since 2020. His philosophy of continuity and trust — blending veterans like Mendes, Vozinha, and Stopira with young diaspora talent such as Livramento and Logan Costa — finally broke through in the nation's seventh qualifying campaign, delivering a first World Cup and a historic run to the knockout stage.
