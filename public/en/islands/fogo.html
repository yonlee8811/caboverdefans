Fogo

A 2,829 m active volcano — wine and lunar landscapes on the Island of Fire.

Fogo

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

Its name means 'fire,' and true to that, the active volcano Pico do Fogo (2,829 m — Cabo Verde's highest point) rises at its center. In the Chã das Caldeiras caldera, volcanic-ash soils produce the famous Fogo wine. The climb is fierce but rewards you with unforgettable, moon-like scenery.
