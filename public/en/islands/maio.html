Maio

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

A laid-back island with few tourists. Its charms are colorful houses, narrow cobbled lanes, and deserted white-sand beaches. A former salt producer, it has a beautiful contrast of dry land and sea. A hideaway for those who simply want peace and quiet.

🏘️

🐦

Salt pans and dry plains rich in birdlife.
