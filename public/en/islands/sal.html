Sal

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

Home to Amílcar Cabral International Airport, Sal is Cabo Verde's main tourism gateway. Its name means 'salt' after its former salt trade. Today it's known for white-sand beaches and resorts, and it's a mecca for windsurfing and kitesurfing. English and euros are widely accepted, making it easy for first-time visitors.
