Santiago

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

The largest island and the political and economic center, home to the capital Praia and a hub for island-hopping. With UNESCO-listed Cidade Velha (the first city Europeans built in tropical Africa), lively markets, the green Serra Malagueta mountains, and beautiful Tarrafal beach, it's often called the 'most African' island — history and nature in one.

✨Highlights

🏛️

Cidade Velha

A World Heritage site — the first European-built city in the tropics, marking the slave-trade era.

🏙️

Praia

The capital's bustling markets and nightlife; a main gateway to the country.

🏖️

Tarrafal

The island's most beautiful beach, framed by palms and hills.
