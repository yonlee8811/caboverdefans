Santo Antão

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

The second-largest and greenest island in Cabo Verde. Its sheer peaks and deep valleys make it the archipelago's hiking mecca. Most visitors arrive by ferry from São Vicente, and traditional rural life still thrives in the mountain valleys where few roads reach.

✨Highlights

🥾

Paúl Valley

A lush green valley dotted with waterfalls and villages — the signature hike of Cabo Verde.

⛰️

Dramatic peaks

Mountains rising straight from the sea and terraced fields — a green contrast to the 'Martian' islands.

🧀

Mountain flavors

Famous for goat cheese, honey, and grogue, the local sugarcane spirit.
