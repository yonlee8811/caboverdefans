São Nicolau

Photo: Cayambe, CC BY-SA 3.0 — Wikimedia Commons

A quiet, unspoiled mountain island untouched by mass tourism. Wind-sculpted rock formations, green valleys, and small colonial-flavored towns are scattered across it. Known as a cradle of Cape Verdean poetry and literature, it suits travelers who want to feel the 'real' Cabo Verde at a slow pace.
