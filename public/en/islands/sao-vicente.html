"If you ain't seen Mindelo, you ain't seen Cabo Verde," as locals say. This is the cultural heart of the country. The port town of Mindelo is the birthplace of morna and coladeira and the home of Cesária Évora, known for its colonial architecture, buzzing streets, and February Carnival.

🎶

Mindelo

The hub of Cape Verdean music — a cultural city full of bars and cafés with live music.

🎭

Carnival

The Brazilian-style February Carnival is the most spectacular in Cabo Verde.

A lovely beach right in town, looking across to Santo Antão.
