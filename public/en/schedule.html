Fixtures — the Blue Sharks' next battles

After their historic knockout-stage run at the 2026 World Cup, the Blue Sharks turn to their next goal: the Africa Cup of Nations 2027. Here's what's coming up.

Please note: Dates and venues are subject to change. Check the official CAF and Cape Verdean Football Federation announcements for the latest. We'll update this page with exact kick-off dates and times as fixtures are confirmed.

🏆Next up: AFCON 2027 qualifiers

The Africa Cup of Nations 2027 — nicknamed "Pamoja" (Swahili for "together") — will be co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania. In the qualifying group-stage draw held in Cairo on 19 May 2026, Cabo Verde were drawn together with Mali, Rwanda and Liberia. The Blue Sharks will play six matches home and away; finishing near the top would send them to a fifth consecutive AFCON finals.
